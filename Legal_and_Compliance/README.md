# Legal and Compliance

## Purpose

The Legal and Compliance repository ensures that the Universal Learning Platform (ULP) is designed, developed, and operated responsibly while meeting applicable legal, privacy, accessibility, security, and educational requirements.

This repository serves as the central reference for legal, ethical, and compliance-related documentation.

---

# Objectives

- Protect learner privacy.
- Support responsible AI development.
- Promote accessibility.
- Respect intellectual property.
- Support regulatory compliance.
- Reduce legal and operational risks.
