# Competitive Intelligence

## Purpose

The Competitive Intelligence repository provides a structured understanding of the competitive landscape for the Universal Learning Platform (ULP).

Its objective is to continuously monitor competitors, identify market opportunities, understand industry trends, and support strategic product decisions.

---

# Objectives

- Understand the competitive landscape.
- Identify market gaps.
- Analyze strengths and weaknesses of competitors.
- Monitor emerging technologies.
- Support strategic planning.
- Inspire innovation while maintaining originality.

# Best Practices

- Base findings on reliable sources.
- Separate facts from opinions.
- Record the date of every analysis.
- Update comparisons regularly.
- Focus on learning rather than copying competitors.
